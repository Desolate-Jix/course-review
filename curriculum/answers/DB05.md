# DB05｜答案与判分要点

先完成[本节练习](../lessons/DB05.md)。答案供核对，不代表唯一正确写法。


```sql
-- E1: 3,4
SELECT u.id FROM users u WHERE NOT EXISTS
(SELECT 1 FROM orders o WHERE o.user_id=u.id AND o.status='paid') ORDER BY u.id;
-- E2: 用户1，总额200
WITH p AS (SELECT user_id,SUM(amount) AS total FROM orders WHERE status='paid' GROUP BY user_id)
SELECT u.id,COALESCE(p.total,0) AS total FROM users u LEFT JOIN p ON p.user_id=u.id
WHERE COALESCE(p.total,0)>=100;
```

E3：需要订单明细用JOIN；只判断用户是否有订单用EXISTS。JOIN可能输出多条匹配，EXISTS只是保留或排除外层行。
E4：与1不同为真，与NULL比较为未知；无法确定不属于整个列表，WHERE不会保留未知结果。
